\textbf{Instructions:}

In the present situation, the websites are not ideal to complete the Jobs-to-be-Done your user has in mind. So let's practice card sorting to rearrange the content of the website!

Organize a card sorting workshop with the content of the website you chose in Ex. 1.

To do so:

\begin{itemize}
\item Define your research goals
\item Prepare the material
\item Find participants
\item Run a card sorting workshop on 5 people
\item Suggest a new taxonomy (content organization)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{List of websites:}

\item More... as long as it is a South American music label ;-)

\textbf{Deliverables:}

\begin{itemize}
\item A card sorting workshop script with the research goals (PDF document)
\item Pictures of the ongoing workshop
\item A document to show the new content organization (it can be a picture of the board with sticky notes or the digital version).
\item Upload the documents on Github, in a zip folder named "ProjectTitle\_Name\_FirstName", with all project deliverables as follows: "Name\_FirstName\_DeliverableName\_Date\_VersionNumber".  For example, the first deliverable can be named as "Doe\_John\_MidFiPrototype\_05242024\_V1".
\item Add a title within all written documents.
\item Pay attention to the global aspect of the deliverables. They must be clear, simple and easy to read. You can get inspiration on canva.com but don't overload your design with too much details!
\end{itemize}
