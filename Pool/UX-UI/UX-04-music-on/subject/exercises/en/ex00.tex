\textbf{Context:}

A music label specialized in music from South America asks you to improve the user experience. This quest is about heuristics and content organization!

\textbf{Instructions:}

Conduct a heuristic analysis on \textbf{one} of these websites:

\begin{itemize}
\item Universal Music Latin America \url{https://www.universalmusica.com/}
\item Delta Records \url{https://deltarecords.net/}
\item Zoho Music \url{https://www.zohomusic.com/}
\item Luaka Bop \url{https://www.luakabop.com/}
\item Rimas Music \url{https://rimasmusic.com/}
\item More... as long as it is a South American music label ;-)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Deliverables:}

\begin{itemize}
\item A duplicated and completed chhecklist on a sheet document (Google Sheets, Airtable, Excel, etc.)
\item Tool : Heuristic evaluation checklist \url{https://drive.google.com/file/d/10KbfbNZA1oVS1sXbjjXLPPmdZ6nqVkdc/view}
\item Upload the document on Github, labeled as follows: "Name\_FirstName\_DeliverableName\_Date\_VersionNumber". For example, it can be labeled as "Doe\_John\_MidFiPrototype\_05242024\_V1".
\item Add a title within the document.
\item Pay attention to the global aspect of the document. It must be clear, simple and easy to read. You can get inspiration on canva.com but don't overload your design with too much details!
\end{itemize}
