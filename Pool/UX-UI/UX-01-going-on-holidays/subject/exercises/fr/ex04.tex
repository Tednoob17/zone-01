\textbf{Instructions :}

To find creative solutions to your problem statement, use one of these techniques in groups of 5 people, twice.

Gather in groups of 2. During the first run, one is the facilitator and the other is the time-keeper and in charge of logistics. Switch roles for the second run.

Get as many ideas as possible within a defined timeframe, and narrow them down to 3 main ideas with a voting process (in pair or in group). Take pictures!

\textbf{Livrables :}

\begin{itemize}
\item A PDF document that includes the script with all the steps + logistics
\item Pictures of the 2 sessions
\item A PDF document with 3 ideas that came out the 2 workshops.
\item Déposez les documents sur Github, dans un zip nommé « ProjectTitle\_Name\_FirstName », et chaque livrable ainsi : « Name\_FirstName\_DeliverableName\_Date\_VersionNumber ». Exemple : « Doe\_John\_MidFiPrototype\_05242024\_V1 ».
\item Ajoutez un titre dans tous les documents écrits.
\item Soignez l'aspect global des livrables. Ils doivent être clairs, simples et lisibles. Vous pouvez regarder canva.com, sans surcharger le dessin de détails.
\end{itemize}
